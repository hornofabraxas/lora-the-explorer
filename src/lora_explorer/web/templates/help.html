How to Play

LoRa the Explorer is a location-based exploration game played over LoRa mesh radio. Grab your spyglass (mobile mesh node), head outside, and survey the world around you.

📡 Commands

Send these as messages to your companion node via the MeshCore app.

/lora survey
Survey your current location. Every survey earns XP, 📦 provisions, and 🪙 survey marks; discovering a new territory pays bonus rewards on top. One survey per territory per day, up to 50 surveys a day (resets 00:00 UTC).
/lora charter
Begin constructing a Survey Post at your current location. Requires a Charter License — rank {{ charter_min_level }} and Base Camp Level {{ charter_min_camp }} ({{ charter_min_camp_name }}). Costs 📦{{ charter_prov_cost }} provisions and 🪙{{ charter_mark_cost }} survey marks, in a discovered territory at least {{ charter_min_dist }} miles from Base Camp. You'll be prompted to name your outpost.
/lora upkeep
Perform upkeep on a Survey Post at your current location — free, but you must physically be there. Restores the post to full income and resets its ruin timer. Surveying past a post no longer maintains it; only upkeep does.

🧭 Core Loop

  1. Survey — explore new territories to earn XP and provisions. Surveying on consecutive days builds Momentum (+5% XP per day, up to +25%); miss a day and it drops a tier.
  2. Upgrade Base Camp — spend provisions to increase your XP multiplier and Survey Post capacity.
  3. Charter Survey Posts — plant outposts in distant territories for passive provision income.
  4. Upkeep — visit your Survey Posts to keep them out of ruin and earning full income.

💰 Currencies

📦 Provisions
Earned from surveys and passive Survey Post income. Spent on Base Camp upgrades, chartering, and upgrading and reinforcing Survey Posts.
🪙 Survey Marks
Earned from every survey — more when you discover new territory. Spent on chartering Survey Posts, Frontier Merchant PvP Supplies, and launching PvP raids.

🚩 Survey Posts

Survey Posts are remote outposts that passively generate 📦 provisions. Higher-level posts and posts further from Base Camp generate more. A post pays full income for 10 days after upkeep, then its income fades to nothing over the next week — falling into ruin.

Ruin never destroys a post or costs it levels — a ruined post just earns nothing until you visit. Restore it with a free on-site /lora upkeep. (On PvP posts, the web Repair button restores battle-damaged Health for provisions.)

📜 Expedition Contracts

Once you reach rank {{ contracts_min_level }} ({{ contracts_min_rank_name }}), the Society offers two weekly Expedition Contracts, randomly drawn from a pool. Spend provisions to accept one, then complete its objective before the week ends (Monday 00:00 UTC) to earn survey marks or a rare relic — the priciest can even award a tier-IV combat munition when PvP is enabled. Uncompleted contracts expire; provisions are not refunded.

🛒 Frontier Merchant

At Base Camp Level {{ merchant_min_camp }} ({{ merchant_camp_name }}), a travelling merchant sets up shop. Buy relics you've previously discovered with provisions, plus a rotating pair of PvP Supplies (attack and defense munitions) for survey marks. Stock is limited per item each week and resets Monday 00:00 UTC.

🖼️ Postcards

Postcards are achievements earned by reaching milestones — distance surveyed, territories discovered, streaks maintained, posts held, and area covered — each class with 5 stars of rising thresholds. A separate Field Training set onboards new explorers; completing it opens the Society Strongbox for 📦200 provisions and a relic.

⚔️ Multiplayer (PvP)

Entirely optional. Register with the war ledger, then enable PvP once you hold a Charter License and at least one Survey Post. On the Warfront you scout rivals — spend a probe to reveal a post's level, age, and defenses — then dispatch attack munitions on a raid. Raids travel 1–12 hours by distance, and each target has a 24-hour cooldown.

Defend by installing defense items on your posts or spending a 🛡️ Wardstone to make one dormant (unraidable) for up to 30 days. Renown — earned from each post's level and how long you hold it — sets your rank on the leaderboard. Only a coarse ~50-mile location and opaque post tokens ever leave your server.
