How to Play

LoRa the Explorer is a location-based exploration game played over LoRa mesh radio. Grab your spyglass (mobile mesh node), head outside, and survey the world around you.

📡 Commands

Send these as messages to your companion node via the MeshCore app.

/lora survey
Survey your current location. Earns XP and 📦 provisions. Discovering a new territory earns bonus rewards and 🪙 survey marks. You can survey each territory once per day (resets at 00:00 UTC).
/lora charter
Begin constructing a Survey Post at your current location. Costs 📦{{ charter_prov_cost }} provisions and 🪙{{ charter_mark_cost }} survey marks. Must be at least {{ charter_min_dist }} miles from Base Camp. You'll be prompted to name your outpost.
/lora upkeep
Perform upkeep on a Survey Post at your current location — free, but you must physically be there. Restores the post to full income and resets its ruin timer. Surveying past a post no longer maintains it; only upkeep does.

🧭 Core Loop

  1. Survey — explore new territories to earn XP and provisions.
  2. Upgrade Base Camp — spend provisions to increase your XP multiplier and Survey Post capacity.
  3. Charter Survey Posts — plant outposts in distant territories for passive provision income.
  4. Upkeep — visit your Survey Posts to keep them out of ruin and earning full income.

💰 Currencies

📦 Provisions
Earned from surveys. Spent on Base Camp upgrades, chartering Survey Posts, and upgrading and reinforcing Survey Posts.
🪙 Survey Marks
Earned from discovering new territories. Spent on chartering Survey Posts and launching PvP attacks.

🚩 Survey Posts

Survey Posts are remote outposts that passively generate 📦 provisions. Higher-level posts and posts further from Base Camp generate more. A post pays full income for 10 days after upkeep, then its income fades to nothing over the next week — falling into ruin.

Ruin never destroys a post or costs it levels; a ruined post simply earns nothing until you visit. Restore full income by physically performing /lora upkeep on-site (free). On PvP posts, the web Repair button restores battle-damaged Health for provisions.

📜 Expedition Contracts

After completing Field Training, the Society offers weekly Expedition Contracts. Two contracts are available each week, randomly drawn from a pool. Spend provisions to accept a contract, then complete the objective before the week ends to earn survey marks or a rare relic — the priciest contracts can even award a tier-IV combat munition when PvP is enabled. Uncompleted contracts expire — provisions are not refunded.

🛒 Frontier Merchant

At Base Camp Level 5 (Lodge), a travelling merchant sets up shop. Purchase relics you've previously discovered using provisions. All purchases are limited to once per week and reset on Monday UTC. Prices are steep — the merchant deals in convenience, not charity.

🖼️ Postcards

Postcards are achievements earned by reaching milestones — distance surveyed, territories discovered, streaks maintained, posts held, and area covered. Each postcard class has 5 stars with increasing thresholds. Collect them all to prove your mastery of the world's edge.
