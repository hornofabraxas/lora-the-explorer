🔒 Privacy & Your Data ▶
What this server stores

This database holds a detailed history of where you have physically been — your base camp coordinates, the latitude and longitude of every survey, and every territory you've discovered. It stays on this machine. The game sends no telemetry and no analytics anywhere.

⚠️ Treat backup files as sensitive — a backup contains your full location history. Don't expose this dashboard to the public internet without protection.

What leaves this server
{% if mp_registered %}

You are registered with the war ledger. It receives your display name, your base camp rounded to about 11 km, and your Survey Posts as opaque tokens — never your precise GPS, your survey history, or your real territory IDs.

{% else %}

Nothing. You are not registered for multiplayer, so no game data leaves this machine.

{% endif %} {% if webhook_url %}

A notification webhook is configured, so PvP alert text is also sent to that URL. Clear it above to stop.

{% endif %}
Your rights

Data on this machine is yours — delete the database or the container at any time. For data held by the hosted war ledger, you can request a copy or deletion. Ask on Discord; requests are handled personally by the maintainer.

The war ledger is 18+.
